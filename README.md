## Project Overview

PowerWise Revolutions SA helps South African households understand their electricity use and find practical ways to manage electricity costs. The website presents information in a clear, accessible way for visitors who may not be familiar with technical energy reports.

## Website Goals and Objectives

- Introduce PowerWise Revolutions SA and its services.
- Share practical ways to save electricity.
- Encourage more efficient energy use.

### Key Performance Indicators

- Receive at least 20 online enquiries during the first month after launch.
- Have 70% of visitors complete the energy assessment form.
- Achieve an average website session duration of three minutes.

## Key Features

- Responsive navigation and layouts.
- JavaScript-generated product and service listings with search and category filters.
- Interactive page features, including FAQs, tips, About Us tabs and an image gallery.
- An inquiry form with validation and an estimated cost and availability response.
- A contact form that validates visitor details and prepares an email draft.
- An interactive Leaflet map with an approximate location marker.
- Search engine metadata and an XML sitemap.

The contact form prepares an email in the visitor’s email app. The visitor reviews and sends the email; the website does not send it automatically.

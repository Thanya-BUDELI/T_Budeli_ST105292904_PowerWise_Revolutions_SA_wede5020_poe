## Project Overview

PowerWise Revolutions SA helps South African households understand their electricity use and find practical ways to manage costs. The website presents information in a clear, accessible way for visitors who may not be familiar with technical energy reports.

## Website Goals and Objectives

### Specific goals

- Introduce PowerWise Revolutions SA and its services.
- Share practical ways to save electricity.
- Encourage more efficient energy use.

### Key performance indicators

- Receive at least 20 online enquiries during the first month after launch.
- Have 70% of visitors complete the energy assessment form.
- Achieve an average website session duration of three minutes.

## Key Features and Functionality

- Responsive navigation and page layouts.
- JavaScript-generated product and service listings with search, category filters, sorting, and details pop-ups.
- FAQ and tips accordions, About Us tabs, and an image gallery lightbox.
- An inquiry form with validation and an estimated cost and availability response.
- A contact form that validates the visitor’s details and prepares an email draft.
- An interactive Leaflet map with a location marker and a location feature.
- Search engine metadata, descriptive image text, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml`.

The contact form opens a prepared email in the visitor’s email app. The visitor reviews and sends it there; the website does not send the email automatically.

## Folder Structure

- `index.html`, `about-us.html`, `products.html`, `services.html`, `inquiry.html`, `contact.html`, `saving-tips.html` — website pages
- `CSS/style.css` — shared website styles
- `JS/` — JavaScript for menus, content, forms, maps, and saving tips
- `Images/` — website images
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` — search engine files
- `_headers` — security headers for Netlify hosting

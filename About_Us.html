About PowerWise Revolutions SA

PowerWise Revolutions SA is a south African Technology centered organisation founded in 
2026 with the goal to assit househols mostly middle to lowerclass use electricity more 
efficiently. This organization keeps the people who are uninterested in understanding the 
complex graphs and data that display how much electricity is being consumed in a way that 
it helps them by providing more easier reports to understand and follow and also provides 
easy ways to make smarter energy decisions

Mission and Vision

  • Mission:  To help south African households reduce electricity costs through simple 
advice.

empowering households to make informed decisions about their energy consumption.
